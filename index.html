01

The fault is invisible from above

A drone captures the top of the glass. A handheld inspection covers what a technician can safely reach on foot — harder going on Suntop's slopes and eroded ground than on flat country like Gunnedah. Every terminal, every MC4, every junction box lives on the one surface neither pass ever actually reaches.

02

One pass beats a campaign

A unit that inspects on every pass produces the same reference points every time — a comparable, dated, geolocated record, instead of the one annual round a drone campaign can realistically deliver.

The cost of a proper look is a crew walking every row with a handheld camera. The value is a dated, geolocated thermal record, referenced to your own CBX and string identifiers.

The obligation

In New South Wales, a hot DC connection is a work-health-and-safety matter, not just a warranty one.

The Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (NSW) and AS/NZS 5033 require DC wiring and connectors to be kept safe and inspected by licensed personnel. SafeWork NSW has run compliance checks at NSW solar farms since 2021, naming DC faults and fire risk explicitly.

Neither sets a threshold or an interval. The standard is whatever you can evidence — a dated, geolocated record of what was found, where, and when. Vegetation encroachment on the same DC infrastructure compounds the same risk — a fault and long grass in the same place is how a fire starts. Every pass leaves a record automatically.

The path

A first look, then the standard travels with the fleet.

01

Trial pilot — thermal inspection only

Five days on site at Suntop — onboarding, geofencing, and a thermal-and-visual pass across the block CalEnergy nominates. A$30,000 fixed, excluding GST, plus mobilisation at documented cost + 10% (estimated A$4,500) — 50% on execution, 50% on commissioning completion. This trial covers thermal inspection only; vegetation management is priced separately as part of the ongoing subscription below.

02

What you get

A geolocated findings report referenced to Suntop's own layout, with MC4 and loom heating reported as its own section — plus Aptics Console access for the trial period.

03

The whole site

150 MW at Aptics' standard per-MW rate (see the calculator above), as a single subscription combining vegetation management and thermal inspection — phased by block once the first look confirms the terrain, at the quarterly pass cadence proposed above. A 3-year term with 5% escalation. The trial fee is credited in full against onboarding if you proceed within 30 days of the report.
